# AI-Driven Biosimulation for Drug Discovery

Netabolics is a techbio company developing a biosimulation platform for drug discovery and repurposing. 

Our [patented](https://patentscope.wipo.int/search/en/detail.jsf?docId=WO2022162440) **virtual cell** technology integrates multi-omics data, ODE-based modeling, and AI-guided simulation, to causally predict the system-wide effects of genetic and pharmacological interventions on cellular biology.
